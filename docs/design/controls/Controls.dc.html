Segmented controls

Tabs in a tool window or document

28 px, muted until selected; the selected tab is bold with a 2 px accent underline. Counts are badges. Kept pages close with their own button.

Lists and grids

Rows of 22 px; headers on their own tint with a sort arrow; a group header carries its count. The selection of a list without the focus is a quieter tint. A keyboard focus without a selection is a 1 px outline. Matches are highlighted, underlined on the selection.

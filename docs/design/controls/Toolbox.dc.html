orderToolTip
  • Click an item or press Space to arm it: its row takes the selection's colors (Shell.Selection, and Shell.Selection.Inactive once the designer has the focus). Click or drag on the form to add it; Esc or the Pointer disarms it.
  • The list never moves: the rows stay where they were when an item is armed. The hint is the designer's status line, a polite live region, so a screen reader still hears it.
  • Double-click an item or press Enter to add it at once, into the selected container, at its top-left corner or a step below and right of the selected control. The designer then has the focus.
  • Drag an item onto the form. Outside the form and in tab order mode the cursor says no drop; a component (a Timer) drops on the component tray. No new tokens and no new icon kinds.

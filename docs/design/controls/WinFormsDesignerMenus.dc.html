For the maintainer to decide

Each as drawn above, with what was rejected. The same questions are in docs/design/controls.md, "The Windows Forms designer's menus (0.6)"; the decision goes into the roadmap's decision register.
1
Show Smart Tag stays, the one entry the classic IDE's menu doesn't have. The panel's property tasks have no menu form; the designer's verbs come first either way. It follows the primary selection: with no verbs it opens the menu alone (f, m: TextBox's smart tag has only Multiline). Rejected: dropping it for exact parity.
2
Align, Make Same Size and Tab Order leave the surface menu, as in the classic IDE; they stay in Format and View. Today's menu is on the right, for comparison; none of its entries has an icon yet. Rejected: keeping them, which the classic IDE's users won't look for there.
3
A component's verbs and Show Smart Tag share the first group, with no separator between them (both are the component's own tasks), on the surface and in the tray (d, i). Verbs keep the designer's text: "Edit Items..." with three periods, "Choose images" in lowercase, as dotnet/winforms writes them. A disabled verb is grayed, not hidden (e: Remove Tab with no tab pages). With more than one component selected there are none (f), as the classic IDE lists verbs for one. Rejected: a separator after the verbs, which the plan's BuildMenuAsync would add; hiding a disabled verb.
4
A locked control keeps its smart tag glyph (l, m, n): locking stops moving and resizing, not the control's tasks, and Show Smart Tag stays in its menu. Rejected: hiding the glyph while locked.

6
The lock adorner is a 12 px tile centered on the frame's top-left corner, where the grab handle it replaces would be (a locked control has none), the panel color with an accent edge like the smart tag's 12 px glyph, and the lock in it at 10 px, close to the classic IDE's 7 × 9 px glyph, which also straddles the corner. It reaches 6 px past the frame, so the form's lies in the surface's 16 px padding (o) and a strip docked at the top covers the window's edge, not the caption (l). The lock is Fluent's lock_closed_16_filled, a new kind (locked, Yellow); the plan draws IconSet.GetFilled("lock") at 12 px at the bounds' −14, −14 and changes to this. Rejected: the first draft's 16 px tile 17 px outside the corner (clipped for the form, over the caption for a docked strip, over any neighbor within 17 px); the bare glyph (the form is drawn in Windows' light colors in every theme, and the dark themes' yellow, #FFCC00, is about 1.3:1 on it); the regular lock, the one GetFilled("lock") compiles, whose 1-unit outline is 0.6 px at this size.

7
Commands that act only on unlocked controls stay enabled when every selected control is locked, as in the classic IDE, and then do nothing: Align to Grid (m, and to Grid in n's Align), Size to Grid and Center in Form. Bring to Front, Send to Back, Cut, Copy and Delete act on locked controls, as there. Rejected: graying them when everything selected is locked, an enablement the classic IDE doesn't have and one more context key.
8
Align's seven entries all get icons (n), in the icon palette's Blue: Fluent's align_left, align_center_vertical (Centers), align_right, align_top, align_center_horizontal (Middles) and align_bottom 16 px regular, six new kinds in fluent-icons.txt, Icon.cs and s_kindColors, mapped from winforms.align.lefts…bottoms, beside to Grid's grid. A submenu has icons on all its entries or none: Order keeps Bring to Front and Send to Back; Make Same Size, Horizontal and Vertical Spacing and Center in Form keep none in this task, TODO(issue). Rejected: to Grid's icon alone in Align (one icon in seven looks accidental); icons for all of Format now (Fluent has only 20 px candidates for sizing, spacing and centering, still to be picked).
9
A tool strip item gets the surface menu until the classic item menu is built (j). The classic IDE's Convert To, Insert, Set Image… and Edit DropDownItems… are deferred, TODO(issue) in the plan's Gaps; meanwhile an item has View Code, Select its strip, the clipboard and Properties, with Bring to Front, Send to Back, Align to Grid and Lock Controls grayed (an item isn't a control). Rejected: no menu on an item until then.
10
The tray's background menu is Paste, then Line Up Icons (h), in the item menu's group order. Rejected: today's Line Up Icons alone.
11
Deliberate differences from the classic IDE: Align to Grid stays enabled (Codalus has snap lines and an 8 px grid but no SnapToGrid layout mode, which is what enables it there); the tray keeps Rename (F2); Lock Controls is left out of the tray menu: the classic IDE disables it for anything but controls, and whether its tray menu lists it at all hasn't been verified. Shortcuts are the key bindings' labels: Delete, not Del.
12
Today's Dock text stays as it is in this task (a, k, l): RenderLayoutAdorners draws "Dock: Fill" or "Dock: Top" in 10 px accent text inside a docked control's bottom left, so on a menu strip it sits over File, and on the form's light colors the accent is under 4.5:1 outside Classic Blue and Light and Harbor Light (Classic Dark 3.1, Harbor Dark 2.8, Classic High Contrast 1.5, Harbor High contrast 1.7; Classic Light 4.2 on the strip). A known overlap and contrast gap of the current surface, TODO(issue): leave it out on strips that take Type Here, and draw it on a panel-colored tile like the lock.

# M34h checkpoint: the gallery review's findings

Date: 2026-10-18. The maintainer reviewed M34's gallery (M34d's `shell.galleryEmpty` and `shell.gallery` steps, with M34f's and M34g's additions) and listed findings on six surfaces, in every theme and on every OS. M34h fixes them and photographs the next gallery in seven themes. No new ADR: the choices follow ADR 0019 (look and feel) and [ADR 0077](../adr/0077-theme-families.md) (the families' tokens). Buttons and inputs, Find Results, HTTP Response, the WCF Test Client, the checklist dialog, AI Chat's overall look and Options wait for the approved mockups of the new control style; they aren't changed here.

## The gallery in seven themes

The maintainer decided that the gallery photographs Classic and Harbor: Classic's Blue, Light, Dark and High Contrast and Harbor's Light, Dark and High contrast (Harbor is the family new installs start with, [design](../design/shell-themes.md)). `shell.galleryEmpty` and `shell.gallery` take the seven (`SmokeTest.UxPass.cs`, `s_galleryThemes`); `CODALUS_GALLERY_THEMES=all` still adds the other families. `index.md` names the themes and has a column per theme by its title. The pictures keep their names, `{surface}-{state}-{theme}.png` by the theme's id (`nuget-data-harbor.dark.png`).

## The control set (in progress)

The maintainer's other request from the same review, "All buttons and inputs should have a common look and feel. Most buttons are generic/bland. Most buttons that can be icon buttons should be converted or have them as Icon + text if needed.", became a design ([`design/controls.md`](../design/controls.md), with mockups of the set and of the redesigned views), approved on 2026-10-18 with every open question as proposed (D33). This part builds its foundation; the views (Find Results, HTTP Response, the WCF Test Client, AI Chat, Options and the plain-button tool windows) follow on it. How a view uses it: [`ui-guidelines.md`](../ui-guidelines.md#the-control-set).

**Built:**
- **Tokens** (`Theming/Tokens.axaml`): every color of the design's table in all sixteen themes (Classic and Harbor the design's values; Graphite, Daylight and Ember mixed from their own colors by the rules in the design, which now lists their values), the sizes and shapes (`Shell.Size.Control` 28, `Shell.Control.CornerRadius` per family, the focus ring's, the dialog's), and a few the build needed: `Shell.Check.CornerRadius`, `Shell.Focus.Margin` and `.CornerRadius`, `Shell.Padding.DialogFooter`, `Shell.Size.Dialog.MaxWidth`, `Shell.Row.Hover.Outline` and `Shell.Chip.BorderThickness` (High contrast's outlines).
- **Control themes** (`Theming/Controls.axaml`, in the application's resources, so they replace Fluent's): `Button`, `ToggleButton`, `DropDownButton`, `CheckBox`, `RadioButton`, `ToggleSwitch` and the new `SegmentedControl`. A plain button has the look without a class; the classes `accent`, `subtle`, `icon` and `iconText` (with `controls:ButtonIcon.Kind`), `compact`, `dialog` and `link` pick a kind; the focus ring is 2 px in `Shell.Focus`, 1 px outside.
- **Styles** (`ShellStyles.axaml`): text boxes (placeholder, hover, focus edge, errors under the field with the icon, read-only, disabled), `TextBox.search`, combo boxes and their lists, split buttons, toolbar buttons and toggles in the toolbar tokens (a checked toggle is a tint with an accent edge), tabs inside a view (28 px, accent underline, badges), rows (hover, an inactive selection without the focus, the keyboard's outline; `ListBox.rows`), chips and badges, the empty state, the dialog's footer.
- **The in-window dialog:** a header (an icon tile in the command's color, the 14 px title, a context line, a close button), the body at 20 px, a footer on `Shell.Dialog.Footer` with a keyboard hint and the buttons in the OS's order (the primary first on Windows and Linux, last on macOS; one primary, the default), the scrim; 520 px wide, growing to 720, and the preview of changes above inside that frame at its own size. The checklist is the mockup's: a bordered list with Select all and its count, member icons, move up and down as icon buttons, Space and Alt+Up and Down. Refactorings that ask first pass their icon and file.
- **The gallery:** the control set's page (`Infrastructure/ControlGallery.cs`) in all sixteen themes on purpose (`controls-set-*`, one window, whatever the gallery's theme list), the checklist in macOS's button order (`dialog-checklist-macos-*`) and a dangerous confirmation (`dialog-confirm-*`) in the gallery's seven themes.

## AI Chat on the control set

The finding on `aichat-data`, "The chat itself needs to be better looking, its just plain text now", answered as [`AiChat.dc.html`](../design/controls/AiChat.dc.html) draws it (approved with D33, the switch for Agent mode included). Only the view changed: what is sent, when and to whom is `AiService`'s as before, AI stays off until it is turned on, and nothing goes before a provider is chosen.

**Built:**
- **The conversation** (`Views/AiChatTurnView`): your question as a bubble on `Shell.Chat.User` at the right (at most 86 % of the width, outlined in High contrast), what went with it under it as chips (**Sent:** a chip a part, **Not sent:** a chip with the reason in its tooltip, the secrets redacted); the answer beside the assistant's avatar (the sparkle on `Shell.Status.Info`), with the model and the time, an **Agent** chip on agent turns, its steps, the answer as Markdown and how it ended (waiting, writing, an error with Configure, stopped, the length limit). The question, the paragraphs, headings, list items and steps can be selected and copied.
- **Markdown** (`MarkdownRenderer` with `MarkdownOptions`): headings at a tool window's sizes (16, 14, 13, 12 px), nested lists (• then ◦), inline code as `Run.code` (the code font on `Shell.Badge`, in every Markdown view now), links as before.
- **Code blocks** (`Views/AiCodeBlockView`): a card whose header row (`Shell.CodeBlock.Header`) names the language (C#, JSON…, and the file a `path=` names) and has Copy and Insert at Cursor as icon buttons and Apply… as an icon-and-text button, 24 px toolbar buttons that wait while the answer is written; the code in a `CodeViewer` in the editor's colors (`EditorColors`) and the new `FitsContent` (as high as its code, no line numbers, long lines scroll sideways), released when its turn leaves the view. The buttons keep their names and do what they did (Apply… through the preview).
- **Steps** (`ViewModels/AiStepItem`, `Views/AiStepView`), a template any agent's steps can use: the status (a check, a clock, an error, a square: a shape each, so color doesn't carry it alone) and the kind (the Agent Client Protocol's read, edit, delete, move, search, execute, think, fetch and other, and the built-in agent's build and MCP tools) as icons; the title (a tool's name in the code font); the files it worked on as links that open them, at a line when there is one (`IAiChatHost.OpenAiLocationAsync`); what else it worked on; how it ended (a failure in the error text color); and details that open under it with a chevron. The built-in agent's tool calls map onto it (`AiStepItem.From`); a step whose outcome only repeats what it read (`read_file`'s path) shows none. They sit in a rail at the left of the answer.
- **The composer**: the context as chip toggles (`ToggleButton.chip`, new in the control set: 22 px pills with a 12 px icon, checked in the toolbar's tint with its accent edge), the line of what goes, then one field (`Border.composer`, new) holding the question (a multi-line box, the placeholder *Ask about your code*), the hint *Enter sends · Shift+Enter new line* and Send, the primary icon button, which is Stop while the chat's turn runs (`AiChatViewModel.IsAnswering`: this chat's turn, where Stop used to follow any AI request, inline suggestions' too). The keys are as they were: Enter sends, Shift+Enter starts a new line.
- **The toolbar**, as the design's: New Chat (`chat-add`), the provider and model as a drop-down (trimmed, the whole label in its tooltip), and at the right end (`Border.toolbarSpacer`, which `ToolbarPanel` now gives the width left) Agent mode as the switch, the request log and Configure. At 280 px the switch, the log and Configure go into the overflow menu, the switch as a checkable entry.
- **Empty states** (an icon, a sentence, the next step): AI off (the existing message and Turn On AI Assistance, the primary), no provider (Choose Provider…), ready (what to ask, and what Agent does). AI turned off with a conversation on screen keeps it, with Turn On in the composer's place.
- **Long conversations**: the turns are a virtualized list (`VirtualizingStackPanel`): a turn is built when it comes into view and released (its code blocks too) when it leaves. The view follows the end while you are there and stays where you scrolled to otherwise (`FollowTail`).
- **Icons**: `chat-add`, `send`, `insert` (Fluent's arrow_import) and `document-check`, with their palette colors.
- **Accessibility**: a name on every control, the steps read as sentences ("read_file Program.cs, done"), the turn's status a polite live region, the avatar hidden from screen readers; Tab goes through the toolbar, the conversation, then the composer.

**Where it differs from the mockup:** the code is in the editor's font one point smaller than the editor's (12 px by default, as drawn); a long line's horizontal scroll bar is the theme's thin one that grows under the pointer; the Selection chip has the select icon, not Insert at Cursor's arrow, and Build output the build icon; the built-in agent's `read_file` reports the file it read, not "54 lines", so its step ends with the file. The model picker and the switch stay in the toolbar, where the mockup puts them.

**For M48 (external agents, ACP):** an agent picker goes after the provider picker (a `DropDownButton.toolbar` like it), the external-agent label in the composer over the question (where the line of what goes is), an agent's tool calls become `AiStepItem`s (kind, title, status, locations and details are ACP's fields) drawn by `AiStepView`, and `AiChatTurnItem.Author` names the agent beside its answers.

**Tests:** `AiChatViewTests` (8), against the mock model server: the bubble (its color, at the right, at most 86 %), the chips of what was sent, the avatar, the model, Markdown (a heading's size and level, inline code's background, nested lists, a link) and the agent turn's step; the code block's header (C#, its color), the editor's colors and its height, Copy (the clipboard), Insert at Cursor (at the caret) and Apply… (through the preview); the chips, the Agent switch, Send (disabled until there is text, Enter sends, Shift+Enter is a new line) and Stop while the turn runs; the empty states (off, no provider, ready, off with a conversation); the step template with the built-in agent's steps and an external one's (icons, the code font, the failure's color, names, links, details) at 280 px; every check of `PolishTests` (cut-off text, names, palette icons, text over text, contrast as rendered) in all sixteen themes at 280 and 556 px; Tab in reading order; and a 30-turn conversation that builds a few turns and shows its end. `PolishTests.Overlapping` no longer counts a link inside a paragraph as text over text (rendered Markdown's inline links). The smoke test's `ai.chat` step asks a second question in agent mode in BuildAndRun, so the gallery's AI Chat has a step (`ai-chat-agent.png`), and `shell.gallery` photographs AI Chat with AI on (back off after), docked and at the design's 556 × 800 in a window of its own (`aichat-design-data-*`), to compare with the mockup's pictures.

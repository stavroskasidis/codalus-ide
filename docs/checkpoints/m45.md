# M45 checkpoint: theme families

Date: 2026-10-17. Phase 11's theme milestone, taken up while M43 is in progress. The designs, with every color: [`design/shell-themes.md`](../design/shell-themes.md). Decisions: D32 (decided 2026-10-16) and [ADR 0077](../adr/0077-theme-families.md), which amends ADR 0019's themes as tokens. Next: M46 (the first-run setup that offers the families).

## Done

**Twelve themes** (`Theming/Tokens.axaml`, `ThemeService`):
- **Harbor, Graphite, Daylight and Ember**, each Light, Dark and High contrast, as Avalonia theme variants (`ThemeService.HarborLight`…), each inheriting Classic's variant of its kind (Light, Dark, Classic's High Contrast), so Fluent's controls take the kind's palette and High contrast keeps Classic's black menus and outlined buttons.
- Each variant's dictionary sits next to Classic's in `Tokens.axaml`'s `ThemeDictionaries` (a merged file would lose to the Classic variant it inherits: Avalonia searches a dictionary's own theme dictionaries first) and sets every `Shell.*` token itself, Dock's brushes, and the Fluent keys where the family's colors show: text boxes and combo boxes (`Shell.Input`), list and tree selection, check boxes, toggle switches and accent buttons, and in High contrast white borders with the accent as focus.
- **Every color of the design**, copied from its tables; what the tables leave out takes the family's panel colors (the design's rule) or a mix of the family's own colors that meets every contrast pair. The tests read the design's tables.
- **Classic:** Blue, Light, Dark and High Contrast, unchanged; the new tokens take, in Classic, the colors it drew before.

**New tokens** (all sixteen themes; [ui-guidelines](../ui-guidelines.md#tokens)):
- the design's: `Shell.Selection.Foreground`, `Shell.Input`, `Shell.Editor.CurrentLine`, `Shell.Editor.Breakpoint`, `Shell.Editor.Breakpoint.Line`, `Shell.Editor.CurrentStatement` (the breakpoint margin and the debugger's line highlights now draw these, `DebugColors`);
- found applying it: `Shell.StatusBar.Empty.Foreground`, `.Empty.Hover`, `.Debugging.Foreground`, `.Debugging.Hover` (Ember's bar is the window's color with dark text and white only while debugging; a High contrast bar without a solution is black under white text); `Shell.TitleBar.Hover`, `Shell.TitleBar.Chip` (Harbor Light's navy title bar: the menu bar's items, their hover and the solution's name on it); `Shell.Search.Foreground` (the search box's text there); `Shell.Toolbar.Start`, `.Foreground`, `.Icon` (Daylight's Start button, filled with the accent).

**Shapes** (the design's [Shapes](../design/shell-themes.md#shapes) table, as values in each theme's dictionary that `ShellStyles.axaml` applies; no view changed):
- the gutter between docked windows (`Shell.Gutter`, `Shell.Gutter.Background` on Dock's `ProportionalStackPanelSplitter`): Harbor 6 px and Ember 8 px of window, Graphite and Daylight a 1 px hairline, Classic Dock's 4 px;
- Ember's cards: 10 px corners on the tool windows' caption and content and the document well (`Shell.Panel.CornerRadius.Top`, `.Bottom`, clipped);
- document tabs: Daylight's and Ember's rounded tabs (Dock's own `DockDocumentTabItemCornerRadius` and `DockDocumentTabItemMargin`, now per theme), and the active tab's indicator on Dock's `PART_TabGutter` (`Shell.Tab.Active.Indicator`, `.IndicatorBrush`): Harbor's 3 px accent bar on top, Graphite's 2 px underline, Ember's 1 px edge;
- the focused tool window: Harbor's accent caption (as before), a bold title elsewhere (`Shell.Caption.Active.FontWeight`), with Graphite's accent dot (`Shell.Caption.Active.Marker`, a drawing brush behind the title, with `.MarkerPadding`);
- the status bar: filled in Harbor, Graphite and Daylight; Ember's in the window's color with a state dot (`Shell.StatusBar.ShowDot`, an ellipse in the status bar: the accent, muted without a solution, white while debugging), orange only while debugging;
- the toolbar: Graphite's in the title row's color (its tokens), Daylight's Start button filled with the accent.

**Editor colors** (`EditorTheme.For`): a family's variant is its kind's colors with the family's C# classification colors (keywords and modifiers, types, methods, strings, numbers, comments, and Razor's markup that shares them) and current line over them. TextMate grammars follow the kind (`TextMateSupport.ThemeFor`); the terminal keeps its kind's ANSI palette with the family's editor background and text. Fonts and Colors still goes over all of it: a family's name (`"[Harbor]"`) applies to its three variants, the variant's (`"[Harbor Dark]"`) over it, and Classic's names stay (`"[Dark]"`, `"[High Contrast]"`).

**The setting and the migration** (D32): `workbench.colorTheme` takes `harbor`, `graphite`, `daylight`, `ember` or `classic` (following the system) or with `.light`, `.dark`, `.highContrast` (Classic also `.blue`). The values from before (`followSystem`, `blue`, `light`, `dark`, `highContrast`) read as Classic and stay valid values, so a saved setting keeps its look and isn't reported; the file isn't rewritten, so an older version still reads it. The default is `harbor`: an install without the setting gets Harbor following the system. VS Code's built-in themes import as Classic's (`classic.dark`…).

**Following the system:** within the family, the OS's high contrast first, then its light or dark; `PlatformSettings.ColorValuesChanged` re-applies it live. `ThemeService.SystemColors` stands in for the OS in tests.

**Choosing:**
- **Tools → Options → Environment → Appearance → Color Theme** (`ColorThemeSettingViewModel`): the families as cards, each with a picture drawn from its tokens in the chosen mode (the title bar and search box, the toolbar and Start, the editor with its current line, the gutter, a panel with its caption and selection, the status bar), a **mode** (Follow System, Light, Dark, High Contrast; Classic adds Blue), and a plugin's theme from its own list. A card keeps the mode where the family has it. A value from before shows as Classic. A `ThemeVariantScope` per card drew nothing when its variant was the window's, so the view model reads the colors instead.
- **Tools → Color Theme** (the quick pick): each family following the system and in each variant, Classic's, then the plugins'; moving previews (`ThemeService.Preview`), Enter saves, Escape goes back; a saved `dark` shows as Classic Dark.
- The Start page's Get Started line names the theme ("Harbor, Follow System").

**Context keys:** `theme` stays the kind (`light`, `dark`, `highContrast`, Classic's `blue`), so plugins' `when` clauses keep working; **`themeFamily`** (`harbor`… `classic`) is new ([plugins](../plugins.md#changelog)).

**Plugins:** a contributed color theme applies over Classic's variant for its `uiTheme`, as before; `themeFamily` is `classic` while it's in use. The plugin API didn't change (no `PublicAPI.txt` change).

**Smoke test:** step `shell.themeFamilies` (M45, after `language.ready`): over the open BuildAndRun solution with the editor focused, each of the twelve variants applies live with its own window color, variant and editor colors, `smoke-test-theme-<family>.<variant>.png`; Harbor follows the system; Options' family picker, `smoke-test-theme-families-options.png`. The gallery photographs all sixteen themes with `CODALUS_GALLERY_THEMES=all` (new; [environment variables](../environment-variables.md)); on CI it stays at Classic's four, as before.

**Docs and site:** ADR 0077; the user guide's [themes](../user-guide/settings.md#themes) (the families, choosing, the setting, accessibility); `ui-guidelines.md` (the checklist's theme lines cover every family, the new tokens, a Shapes table); `plugins.md` (`themeFamily`, contributed themes, the changelog); the design doc's status; the website's Features page (a screenshot of each family from the smoke step, the "Make it yours" list), Home's "Familiar" card and the README; `CHANGELOG.md`.

## Tests

- **`ThemeFamilyTests`** (15, new):
  - unit: the setting's parsing (families, variants, D32's mapping of today's values, plugin ids and invalid values), resolving with the system (light, dark, high contrast, pinned, Classic, the default), the descriptor (default `harbor`, every value, today's values valid, titles), kinds, families and variants (each variant's root is Fluent's Light or Dark of its kind);
  - against the design: its tables parsed for each family; every editor color of each variant in `EditorTheme`, and each meets 4.5:1 (7:1 in High contrast) on the editor and its current line; every `Shell.*` color of every table in every variant's resources;
  - headless: every family dictionary sets every `Shell.*` key Classic has, and every token the shell's views and code ask for resolves in all sixteen themes; switching family and variant live with `theme` and `themeFamily` (and a `when` clause on them), the editor's and the terminal's colors; Follow System within a family through light, dark and high contrast, and a pinned variant staying; a setting saved before M45 (`blue` in the file before the shell starts, then `dark`, `highContrast`, `light`, `followSystem`) with Classic's colors and the file unchanged; color customizations by family and variant; the quick pick (every variant listed, preview without saving, a legacy value shown as Classic, Enter saves); Options' cards (a card, a mode, Classic's Blue, a family without Blue, a legacy value shown as Classic, each picture's colors and shapes, the chosen card drawn when its variant is the window's); each family's shapes in the window (gutters, Ember's corners and dot, Daylight's Start, Harbor Light's navy title bar and readable menu bar, Classic's unchanged).
- **`PolishTests`:** the contrast pairs in all sixteen themes, text at 7:1 in the families' High contrast variants; `contrast-pairs.txt` gained the design's pairs (text on the selection and on text boxes, the search box, the title bar's hover and chip, the Start button, the status bar's states and their hover, the editor's current, breakpoint and current-statement lines, the breakpoint glyph); the clipped-text walk at 280 px now runs in Classic and in each family.
- **Updated:** `IconColorTests`, `UxPassTests` and `ChromeTests` walk all sixteen themes (`ThemeService.Themes`, High contrast by `IsHighContrast`); `VsCodeImportTests` expects `classic.light`.
